Hey, y'all

Some families collect souvenirs. We collect campsites.

We're James and Mallory, with our toddler and our dogs — including the one who inspired it all, Willow.

We've been camping since 2007, graduated from tents to trailers, and made the leap to our first 5th wheel in 2025. We don't live on the road full-time — we just refuse to let vacation season be the only time we make memories.

This is our little corner of the internet for families who love the outdoors, aren't afraid of a little adventure, and want real, practical advice from people who've done it with kids, dogs, and a fully packed truck.
